    Rules. Everyone starts with 100 coins and 5 random letters. Each round one letter from the bag gets sealed bids; the highest bid wins it. In a Vickrey auction the winner pays the second-highest bid, in a first-price auction their own. Words earn coins and points at once: letter values times a length bonus (1× up to 3 letters, 3× for 7+), which is why strong bots save up for 7-letter words.

    Live match. Nijello's current real match, a second or two behind. Other bots' bids are sealed until the match ends, so only the price paid shows; each bot's starting letters show as "?" until it plays them.

    Who's playing in the showcase. Named opponents are models of the arena's top bots, built from their public match history: they bid like the real bot's past winning bids and favour the word lengths it plays. They are approximations, not the bots themselves. "Field model" bids like the average real bot; sims and starter kits are the server's simple bots. Showcase matches are extra games, slowed down so you can follow them; they don't affect training.
